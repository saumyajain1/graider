Privacy policy

Graider is a personal project maintained by Saumya Jain. This policy describes the information used by the hosted Graider app and how to contact its maintainer.

Information Graider stores

Account information includes your name, email address, and sign-in records. Password accounts store a password hash, not the original password. When you use Google, Graider also stores your Google account identifier and verified-email information.

Your workspace contains the assignments, uploaded files, questions, reference answers, rubrics, student names or identifiers, submissions, scores, and feedback you provide or generate. Graider also records AI usage and request metadata to enforce limits and troubleshoot failures. Hosting services may record connection information, such as IP addresses and request paths, in operational logs.

Google sign-in and account linking

Google sign-in requests only basic identity information: your name, email address, and profile. Graider uses this information to create or authenticate your account, display your name, and connect Google to an existing account when you explicitly choose to do so.

Graider does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, or calendars. It does not receive your Google password or store Google access or refresh tokens. Signing out of Graider does not sign you out of Google. You can revoke the app's authorization in your Google account connections.

How information is used and shared

Graider uses your information to operate your account and workspace, process uploads, prepare and grade assignments, export results, enforce usage limits, and maintain the service. The maintainer can access stored information when needed to operate the service or handle support requests.

The hosted app uses Render for the web service and Neon for its database and private file storage. Google processes sign-in requests. The workspace loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives browser connection information when the fonts are requested.

When you use an AI feature, relevant assignment, question, reference-answer, rubric, or submission text is sent to OpenAI for processing. Graider does not add your Google account identifier or account email to AI prompts, but identifying information included in your assignment or submission text will also be sent. Use sample work and avoid including real student information.

These providers handle information according to their own policies: Render, Neon, Google, and OpenAI. Graider does not sell account information or add advertising or analytics trackers.

Cookies

Graider uses session cookies to keep you signed in and a security cookie to protect account actions against forged requests. Blocking these cookies can prevent sign-in and other account features from working.

Retention and deletion

Account and workspace information remains stored until it is removed. You can delete assignments and their associated submissions and uploads in the app. For account deletion or other privacy requests, contact the maintainer below. Removing Google's authorization alone does not delete your Graider account or workspace.

Providers may retain backups and operational logs under their own retention policies. Removing data from the active app does not guarantee immediate removal from every provider backup.

Contact

For privacy questions or account-deletion requests, email saumyajain1403@gmail.com. Use the email associated with your Graider account when requesting changes; ownership must be verified before account data is removed.

Policy updates

Changes to how Graider handles information will be reflected on this page, with an updated date.
